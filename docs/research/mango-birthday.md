# Mango birthday adaptation
Reference: https://noel.noel-christmas.workers.dev/
Observed: full-screen nighttime 3D village, warm illuminated windows, walking characters, ambient particles, floating controls. Original has networked visitors and join dialog.
Requested adaptation: simpler offline birthday scene, existing Mango mascot, entering a house, presents, candle blowing. Keep existing config.js birthday text.
Interaction: click-to-progress, automatic puppy walk during door transition, three clickable gifts, cake blow button, final letter and replay. Keyboard accessible buttons. No multiplayer or login.
Implementation: dependency-free HTML/CSS/JS, layered miniature house with perspective floor and warm light, existing mascot PNGs, mobile scene scaling, reduced-motion support.
